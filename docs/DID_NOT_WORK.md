# What did not work (one honest sentence each)

- The cloud sandbox and the Linux shell on the laptop cannot reach PyPI or GitHub (egress policy 403), so emulators run only in the Windows venv through `scripts/win_runner.bat`; everything else is developed against pulser-core copied from that venv.
- CPLEX is not installed in the hackathon venv and could not be installed in one try, so the §9.3 hierarchy is brute force (exact set-partition DP, proven optimum) cross-checked by simulated annealing, not CPLEX.
- A hand-typed baseline number (P(reach ref) after repair = 0.066, actually measured on a toy instance) slipped into DERIVATIONS.md/STATUS.md and was caught by the reviewer; the correct S_seed00 value is 0.080 and numbers now come only from run records.
- Runner job 002 (`device_limits.py --assert-brief`) crashed on Windows with `UnicodeEncodeError: 'charmap' codec` (cp1252 console, printing `δ`); re-queued as 007 with `set PYTHONUTF8=1`, which every later `.cmd` now sets — the running `win_runner.bat` was not edited because cmd reads batch files incrementally.
- No single load-balance weight `λ₂` makes `onehot-ub` sound on all 40 toy tier-S seeds (max 35/40 at κ=0.15): on seeds where capacity binds, "closest" and "most balanced" conflict and a soft term cannot force `L_k ≤ Q`; we rely on reporting the best *feasible* shot (brief Pitfall 9.1) and say so in MODEL.md §6.
- The emu-mps timing calibration job (12/20/30/40 atoms × χ 16/32/64, 200 shots) did not finish in 55 min on the laptop CPU and blocked the serial job queue; it was killed and will be re-queued last with a smaller grid and unbuffered output.
- `pulser_simulation.QutipConfig(noise_model=None)` crashes (it dereferences the argument before its base class replaces None); passing no `noise_model` at all is the fix — found by the first Windows pytest run, 111/112.
- qubo-solver's classical `simulated_annealing` (0.9.1) returns the same non-optimal string `10010110` on all three tier-S onehot-ub instances, with 20 000 or 200 000 proposals and with pre/post-processing on or off (model gap 78–118 %), while its `tabu_search` finds the optimum at once; we therefore use `qubosolver-tabu` as the qubo-solver classical baseline and report the SA rows as what they are.
- The one-hot embedding optimiser (`cvrp/embed_onehot.py`) is seeded but not bit-identical across platforms: the same seed gave δ_f = 32.8 rad/µs in the sandbox and 35.1 rad/µs on Windows (P(reach) 51 % vs 90 % on S_seed00). Registers of every reported row are therefore cached in `embeddings/` and committed; the metric differences between the two solutions are themselves a finding (the fit objective does not fully predict P(reach)).
- The one-hot rook's graph does not embed for K = 3 (tier M, 30 atoms): with 5 µm triangles the one-hot-subspace fit residual is 38 (tier S: 0.06–0.32), single-flip defects fall below their parents and the model optimum ranks ~10 000th of 59 049 one-hot strings; opening the triangles to 6–10 µm lowers the energy scale (U_clique 2–19 rad/µs, δ_f 1–10 rad/µs) without fixing the ordering (rank ≥ 1 279) and pushes the register to the 38 µm radius — a limit of one-hot encodings on a 2-D 1/r⁶ register, not of the weights.
- A 12-point scan of the penalty weights (A × {0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5}, λ₂ × {0.5, 1, 2}) showed no trend because the embedding optimiser lands on a different local minimum for every weight setting; the weights were left as derived.
- Syncing the sandbox repo to the Windows folder by tarball overwrite silently reset edits a parallel chat session had made in the working tree (15:45); replaced by git patches applied in both directions (`git format-patch` / `git am`), with trees checksum-compared after each exchange.
- The tight exactly-one weight `A = R_w/K` (`onehot-ub-tight`), although provably sound for the QUBO, loses most of the device's margin: with the same register and pulse P(reach optimum) drops from 0.94 to 0.03 on S_seed00 and from 0.95 to 0.52 on S_seed01 (S_seed04 0.89 → 0.94; qutip, in-house SV: 0.03 / 0.56 / 0.96), so the headline keeps the larger provable `A` and `tight` is reported as the ablation.
